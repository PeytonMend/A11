Andrew Ross Sorkin

Andrew Ross Sorkin is a journalist, author, and financial columnist for The New York Times. He is also the founder and editor of DealBook, a publication that covers business and finance.

Sorkin is known for reporting on major events in the business world. He wrote the book Too Big to Fail, which examines the 2008 financial crisis.
